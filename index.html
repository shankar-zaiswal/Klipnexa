ABOUT KLIPNEXA

Make space for
the creative part.

Klipnexa Studio is an independent software project exploring simpler video creation workflows for creators, teams and businesses.

Our goal is to make useful tools that are approachable, practical and built around real content needs. We’re currently preparing the first version and welcoming conversations with people who would like to try it.

The person behind Klipnexa
✦ Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Shankar Jaiswal.

Founder of Klipnexa

I believe creating educational videos shouldn't feel like a complicated production process or require hours of editing before you can share an idea.

That's why I'm building Klipnexa — to make turning educational content and ideas into engaging Shorts simpler and more approachable, with ready-made templates and a clearer creation workflow.

My professional background in business intelligence, MIS and business analysis has shaped my interest in practical tools that make everyday work easier. I bring that same mindset to Klipnexa, with the goal of making video creation more convenient for people using a laptop or mobile device.
